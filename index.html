Prepress FAQ

What file format should I send?+
Print-ready PDF (PDF/X) is best, or vector AI/EPS. High-res TIFF works for photos. We check every file before press.
Do you convert RGB to CMYK?+
Yes. We convert and soft-proof in CMYK, then confirm on a proof before the run.
How long does a print job take?+
Most jobs are ready in 3–5 business days after proof approval, depending on quantity and finish.
Can you deliver outside Agadir?+
Yes. Pick up in Agadir or we ship anywhere in Morocco.
